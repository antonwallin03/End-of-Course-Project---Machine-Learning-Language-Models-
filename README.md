# End-of-Course-Project---Machine-Learning-Language-Models-
This is my end of course project for the course Machine Learning &amp; Lanugage Models at Universiteit van Amsterdam in 2026. Together with my partner, we compared BERT sentence embeddings to fMRI data from Pereira et al. (2018). The comparison was made across three brain regions and two linguistic features. 
The project was split into three parts. The first part was the completion of a Python notebook, which everyone from the course had to do. The second was an extension on said notebook, where we got to investigate a custom research question, drawing on the methods and themes of the notebook. Lastly, we wrote a four page project report, which summarises our project and our extension findings. 
Overall, the project expanded my confidence in ML and statistical methods. I got to implement course material hands-on in Python, which I enjoyed. Additionally, I got to relate my background in Linguistics to NLP, ML, and Neuroscience, incorporating knowledge from my Bachelor's courses such as Neurolinguistics and Research Methods & Statistics.
